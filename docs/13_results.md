## Discussion

The model achieved 79.43% accuracy and 80.12% macro F1-score on the 282-image held-out test set. Denim produced the strongest F1-score (86.31%), while silk produced the lowest (75.27%), indicating that visually similar or reflective textile surfaces remain challenging. The non-fabric class supports safer operation by reducing the chance that unrelated images are treated as garments. The confusion matrix shows that errors occur primarily between visually similar categories rather than uniformly across all classes.

The deployed system couples the model output with a structured care knowledge base. Therefore, washing, drying and ironing guidance is not presented as a direct CNN prediction; it is a transparent rule-based recommendation retrieved after the fabric category is predicted. These results support use as a planning and decision-support prototype. Additional independently collected garment images, blend labels, real-world lighting tests and user-study responses are required before making stronger generalization or usability claims.
